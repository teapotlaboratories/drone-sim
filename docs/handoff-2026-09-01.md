## What is done and works

**`SIM-27` is fixed, harness-side.** The collision witness was polling `simGetCollisionInfo` at
20 Hz; that RPC is read-and-reset, so it consumed the touchdown flag `FastPhysicsEngine` needs to
engage the ground lock. `scripts/watch_collisions.py` now **brackets** the airborne phase with
exactly two reads (up through 2 m, back down) and scores on the monotonic `collision_count`.
Verified: max pose split **1.009 m** across 3 seeds, against 24.75 m before.

**No vendor patch.** An earlier fix (`0012`) patched Cosys-AirSim so its RPC stopped consuming;
the owner correctly objected that patching upstream so *our* tool stops breaking it inverts this
project's primary rule. It was deleted. A test now fails if a collision-ownership patch reappears
in the auto-applied directory.

**The plugin is pristine.** `assets/CitySample`'s plugin is byte-identical to upstream and its
binary md5 (`a77bfe1c`) matches the original build from before any of this work — probes reverted,
`.orig`/`.rej` litter cleared. The three probe patches stay in `patches/cosys-airsim/experimental/`
(logging only, verified line by line) for re-instrumenting later.

**The world is on the NVMe** at `assets/CitySample` (gitignored). Bring-up dropped from 238–283 s
to ~40 s; a whole run from 536–808 s to ~141 s. The 7 TB copy still exists and still carries the
probes plus the abandoned `0012` — stale, harmless, worth re-syncing or deleting.

## What is NOT done

**The site is wrong, and that is the only thing left before a clean gate.** Three failures in a
row were about *where* the aircraft was put, not what the code did:

- a tree over the takeoff point (`SIM-44`) — fixed by moving the spawn to `(-8, -8)`
- a **parked car** under the landing point — NOT fixed; seed 2 of the last gate bounced against a
  car in a parking bay, physics reporting a steady 1.9 m/s descent while the position oscillated
  ±0.1 m, so PX4 never registered touchdown and `LAND` timed out

**The method that found the tree only checks half the problem.** Candidate spawns were surveyed by
pointing the camera STRAIGHT UP from 5 m at the centre and the four extremes of the ±5 m jitter
disc (`out/sim44-up-*.png`). Clear sky was verified; **the ground was never looked at**. The next
step is the same survey pointed DOWN — flat pavement, no cars, benches or planters — and then one
3-seed gate on `citysample-gate1`.
