GPS Waypoints,
Without The Controller

The last of the five capabilities, and the one the design called “the only piece needing controller changes”. It didn’t. A 10 m square expressed in GPS converted back to exactly the same square in metres and flew 4/4 — with offboard_control untouched.

01 Why it looked like a controller job

PX4 has no global setpoint message — verify_nav_interface.py:15 had already recorded it: GotoSetpoint is local NED, and VehicleGlobalPosition is an estimate output, not a command. So a lat/lon has to become a local coordinate somewhere.

The assumption was that “somewhere” is the controller. It is not — it only has to happen before the setpoints start, and run_scenario.py is already there, already talks to the stack, and already writes the provenance.

Every waypoint lands ~14 m off, on a stack where the aircraft, the controller and the estimator are all healthy. The conversion therefore reads ref_lat/ref_lon/ref_alt from /fmu/out/vehicle_local_position — and that reader already existed in check_ekf_origin.py, including the BEST_EFFORT QoS handling a second copy would have had to duplicate, so it gained a --print-ref mode rather than a sibling.

03 Matching PX4’s projection, not a better one

The maths is MapProjection::project — azimuthal equidistant on PX4's sphere, R = 6371000. A more accurate ellipsoid would be worse: the target frame is PX4's, so any disagreement in Earth model becomes a position error the aircraft cannot see.

origin itself        ->  E 0.000   N 0.000    U 0.000
+0.0001 lat, +20 m   ->  E 0.000   N 11.119   U 20.000
+0.0001 lon          ->  E 8.832   N 0.000    U 0.000

8.832 m per 0.0001° of longitude at 37.4° is 111320 × cos(37.4°) × 0.0001. Unit-checked before anything flew.

04 The test is a round trip, deliberately

Declaring arbitrary GPS coordinates would only prove the aircraft flew somewhere. Instead the scenario's waypoints are generated by inverting the projection on the known square, so the conversion has to give the square back:

37.4123278, -121.9947352, 71.28 m  ->  E +10.00  N  +0.00  U +20.00
37.4124177, -121.9947352, 71.28 m  ->  E +10.00  N +10.00  U +20.00
37.4124177, -121.9948484, 71.28 m  ->  E  +0.00  N +10.00  U +20.00
37.4123278, -121.9948484, 71.28 m  ->  E  +0.00  N  +0.00  U +20.00

Exact. Flight: 4/4 waypoints, errors 0.801 / 0.769 / 0.789 / 0.783 m — indistinguishable from the same square declared in ENU, which is the point.

Refused rather than guessed

A scenario declaring both waypoints_enu and waypoints_gps is an error. There is no defined precedence, and picking one silently flies the wrong mission.

Written down rather than guarded

The conversion is pinned at one moment. If PX4 re-initialises its EKF origin mid-flight the waypoints are stale. Acceptable for a single sortie, not for a long one — and a guard nobody has seen fire is not evidence either.
