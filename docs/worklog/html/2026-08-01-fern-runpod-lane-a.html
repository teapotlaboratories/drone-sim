01 · Goal and boundary

What this change is

Contributor model

The original contributor retains carbonite. Fern/Runpod is an option for contributors without access, not a replacement.

Safety

SITL and off-target checks only. No aircraft, HITL, or hardware transport was touched.

Delivery

The requested stopping point is an open pull request. Nothing is merged automatically.

Fern source inspection confirmed deploy --image, mandatory billable-request inspection with --dry-run, explicit --yes, a CPU-only 8-vCPU profile, no mapped ports, and /workspace. Fern currently provides deploy/list/get/stop; wait, download, and guarded destroy remain planned. The image contract moved into Drone Sim while the full PR gate remains out of scope.
